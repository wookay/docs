@logic

To help understanding the bitwise (&, |), short-circuiting booleans (&&, ||).

julia> using LogicalOperators

julia> @logic 3 & 10
   0b0011  3
 & 0b1010 10
   0b0010  2
2

julia> @logic 10 | 7
   0b1010 10
 | 0b0111  7
   0b1111 15
15

julia> @logic 3 & 10 | 7
   0b0011  3
 & 0b1010 10
   0b0010  2
 | 0b0111  7
   0b0111  7
7

julia> @logic 3 & (10 | 7)
   0b1010 10
 | 0b0111  7
   0b1111 15
   0b0011  3
 & 0b1111 15
   0b0011  3
3
